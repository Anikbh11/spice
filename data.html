Data availability

SPICE data

No SPICE datasets are public yet. We are preparing selected catalogues and measurements from the five studies listed below. Download links will be added after the files and documentation have been checked.

01 · Planned datasets

Measurements from five studies.

Planned data products

These releases will contain measurements used in the published analyses, not full simulation snapshots. Contents may change before publication.

  1. 01

    Feedback and reionisation

    Global ionised-fraction histories and summary measurements comparing the three feedback runs.

  2. 02

    Lyα emission

    Mock Lyα spectra and luminosities measured across sightlines and apertures, including Lyα–UV spatial offsets.

  3. 03

    UV luminosity-function variability

    UV magnitudes, luminosity functions, and time-resolved star-formation measurements used to quantify feedback-driven variability.

  4. 04

    [O III] emission

    Integrated [O III] luminosities together with galaxy sizes, morphologies, and line kinematics.

  5. 05

    [C II] emission

    Integrated [C II] luminosities, star-formation rates, emission sizes, and line-profile measurements.

02 · Documentation

What will accompany each dataset.

Files, definitions, and citation

01Data files
Machine-readable tables in a format suited to the product, with units stated in the file or accompanying README.
02Selection and definitions
Redshift, feedback model, sample cuts, apertures, sightlines, binning, and column definitions.
03Citation and version
Dataset version, authors, licence, DOI or repository record, and the paper that defines the measurement.

03 · Citation

Cite the dataset and its paper.

How to cite released SPICE data

Each download page will give a preferred data citation.

Please cite both the data record and the paper listed with the dataset. Until a release appears here, cite the relevant paper only.
