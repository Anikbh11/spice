Project 03 · Galaxy populations

Galaxy statistics during reionisation

Population-level predictions connecting stellar growth and feedback variability to ultraviolet luminosities, galaxy abundance, and the sources of reionisation.

01 · Question

Connecting internal physics to population statistics.

Which galaxies populate the early Universe?

Observed galaxy counts compress complex and variable growth histories into a population-level signal.

SPICE tracks galaxies across matched feedback simulations to study how ultraviolet luminosity functions, stellar masses, star-formation histories, and halo properties respond to the feedback model.

The comparison helps separate robust predictions from quantities that depend sensitively on the timing and energy of stellar feedback.
