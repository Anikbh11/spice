Overview

Scientific context

This project translates SPICE outputs into observable forms: broadband photometry, emission-line predictions, morphology, and survey-like mock products for comparison to current and future observations.

These products bridge theory and observation by placing simulated galaxies into forms that can be compared more directly to real survey data.
