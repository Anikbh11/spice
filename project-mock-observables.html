Project 04 · Theory to observation

Mock observables and synthetic surveys

Translating SPICE outputs into photometry, emission-line predictions, morphology, and survey-facing products for comparison with current observations.

StatusIn progress
ScaleGalaxies to survey catalogues
FocusPhotometry and line emission
FacilitiesJWST, ALMA, VLT

01 · Question

Making like-for-like comparisons.

What would SPICE galaxies look like?

Simulation variables become most useful to observers when they are translated into measurements made by real instruments and surveys.

This project develops survey-facing products such as broadband fluxes, emission-line predictions, image-based morphology, and compact catalogue quantities.

The aim is to preserve the physical information in SPICE while making selection effects, apertures, viewing geometry, and model assumptions explicit.

02 · Products

Connections between theory and measurement.

A survey-facing toolkit

01

Synthetic photometry

Model fluxes and colours through defined instrument filters, paired with the assumptions required to produce them.

02

Emission-line predictions

Line luminosities and ratios that connect feedback-sensitive gas conditions to JWST, ALMA, and VLT measurements.

03

Morphological views

Projected images that expose structure, orientation, and resolution effects rather than reducing galaxies to single integrated values.

04

Catalogue summaries

Documented, versioned tables designed for reproducible comparisons with observational samples.
