The SPICE simulation suite

Impact of stellar feedback on the first galaxies and cosmic reionisation

The advent of the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) has opened a new observational window on the high-redshift universe, allowing us for the first time to directly explore the properties of galaxies at redshifts as high as 15, when the universe was only about 300 million years old. The interpretation of this trove of new data needs to be guided by theoretical models.

The SPICE radiation-hydrodynamics simulation suite was specifically designed to systematically explore a variety of stellar feedback models and their impact on both the reionisation process and the properties of the first galaxies, with the aim of interpreting data from JWST, as well as ground-based telescopes such as the Atacama Large Millimeter Array (ALMA) and the Very Large Telescope (VLT).
