Project 02 · Cosmic topology

Reionisation structure and topology

How ionised regions form, expand, and overlap around the clustered distribution of early galaxies across the cosmic web.

StatusPublished research
ScaleGalaxies to cosmological volume

01 · Question

A patchy transformation of the Universe.

How does reionisation unfold?

Reionisation is not spatially uniform. Ionised regions emerge around clustered sources, expand through their environments, and eventually overlap.

SPICE connects this large-scale morphology to the regulation of star formation and photon escape inside the galaxies that supply the radiation.

Comparing feedback models reveals how small-scale changes in galaxy evolution can alter the timing, topology, and observational signatures of reionisation.

Clustering shapes bubbles

02

Environment changes timing

Dense and underdense regions do not follow identical ionisation histories, creating a spatially extended transition.

03

Topology affects observables

The geometry of ionised and neutral regions is part of the interpretation of Lyα transmission, future 21-cm measurements, and cross-correlations.

Quantitative bubble statistics, redshift comparisons, and observational forecasts should be added directly from the associated analysis rather than generalised from this overview.
